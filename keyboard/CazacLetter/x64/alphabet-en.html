Kazakh Қ is a similar sound,

29 Ш X x The visual shape of X resembles
the Kazakh Аша and Айқыш (both
mean "cross") — a traditional symbol.
Plus, in Chinese Pinyin, X is
pronounced [ʃ] (like "sh"). This
gives Ш a natural Latin representation.
